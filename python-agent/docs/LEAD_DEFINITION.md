# What counts as a lead

The agent works for an Indian software/AI development agency. A lead is a person we can pitch
outsourced engineering to, based on a LinkedIn post that shows a **current** need.

## Good lead (BUYER)
- Founder, co-founder, CEO, CTO, VP/Head of Engineering or Product at a company that is not itself a dev agency.
- Located in the United States.
- Post (within the last ~7 days) shows a live engineering need, such as:
  - building an MVP/app/platform and needing help
  - looking for a dev shop, agency, contractors or a technical partner
  - hiring engineers / a founding engineer / a fractional CTO (budget exists, capacity is short)

## Not a lead
| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| SELLER | agency/freelancer/consultant promoting their own services, "we build MVPs for founders" |
| RECRUITER | recruiters, staffing, headhunters, "hiring for my client" |
| JOB_SEEKER | "open to work", students, people looking for a role |
| INVESTOR_MEDIA | VCs, accelerators, newsletters, podcasts, event promoters |
| CLOSED | role filled, "we found our engineer", job-start announcements |
| NO_NEED | thought leadership, hiring in non-engineering roles, funding news without an engineering ask |
| NON_US | clearly based outside the US |

## Rules of thumb
- Hiring an employee is still a lead (an agency can offer staff augmentation), but weaker than "need an agency / MVP".
- When the post is ambiguous, the judge must say so with low confidence; low confidence is never saved as a lead.
- Every verdict must quote the evidence from the post.
